## Import decision

- **Staged candidate:** the 2,222-row Disease Ontology disease-to-SYMP table, retaining exact IDs and labels, as draft-only associations.
- **Not promoted:** the 2026-08-31 ontology snapshot is older than the AURA 2026-09-30 source already processed; no stale replacement was made.
- **Quarantined from clinical catalogs:** the DDXPlus synthetic benchmark, mixed generated diagnosis/test CSV, and mixed RAG CSV.
- **Not imported:** MedQuAD until rights are verified per source subset; its README states that MedlinePlus ADAM, drugs, and herbs/supplements answers were removed to respect copyright.
- No data was added to live catalogs or made accessible to the API. Existing loader/service gates require clinically reviewed, published records.
- No translation, medical fact completion, missing-value filling, or name-based linking was performed. Blank values remain reported as blanks.

The first five rows of each CSV/TSV were inspected during the local audit. Verbatim row contents are intentionally not duplicated in this report. The inventory lists field names, row counts, and missing-value statistics.
